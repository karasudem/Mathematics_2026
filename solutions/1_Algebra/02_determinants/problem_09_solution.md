Exercise 9 – Solution

Given two vectors:

u = [ 3 ]
[ 1 ]

v = [ 1 ]
[ 4 ]

We need to calculate the area of the parallelogram using a determinant.

Step 1: Form the Matrix

Place the vectors as columns of a matrix:

A = [ 3  1 ]
[ 1  4 ]

Step 2: Calculate the Determinant

For a 2x2 matrix:

det(A) = (a * d) - (b * c)

det(A) = (3 * 4) - (1 * 1)

det(A) = 12 - 1

det(A) = 11

Step 3: Calculate the Area

The area of the parallelogram is the absolute value of the determinant.

Area = |det(A)|

Area = |11|

Area = 11 square units

Step 4: Reverse the Order of the Vectors

If we reverse the order of the vectors, the new matrix is:

B = [ 1  3 ]
[ 4  1 ]

det(B) = (1 * 1) - (3 * 4)

det(B) = 1 - 12

det(B) = -11

Step 5: Compare the Results

The determinant changes from 11 to -11.

However, the area does not change because we use the absolute value of the determinant.

Area = |-11| = 11 square units

Final Answer

The area of the parallelogram is 11 square units.

Reversing the order of the vectors changes the sign of the determinant but does not change the area.
