Exercise 1. Matrix Size and Entries

1. Sizes of matrices A and B

Matrix A has 2 rows and 3 columns.

Therefore, the size of A is 2 × 3.

Matrix B has 3 rows and 2 columns.

Therefore, the size of B is 3 × 2.

2. Matrix entries

In matrix notation, the first subscript indicates the row number, and the second subscript indicates the column number.

The required entries are:
* a₁₂ = -1
* a₂₃ = 5
* b₂₁ = -2
* b₃₂ = 1

3. Rows and columns as vectors

The second row of matrix A is:

(0, 4, 5)

The first column of matrix B is:

(1, -2, 4)ᵀ
